```
Day  0: Email 1 — Problem Hook / ROI Billboard → CTA: 10-min assessment

Day 14: Email 4 — Breakup / Final Offer → CTA: Free resource + open door
```

## Rules for All Follow-Ups

1. **Never resend the exact same email** — always add new information
2. **Threading:** Days 3 follow-ups should use the same subject (or no subject) 
   to keep the thread alive
3. **New thread:** Day 7 and Day 14 should use fresh subjects
4. **Timing:** Send at 8:00 AM local time of the recipient
5. **Personalization:** Every email must have at least one personalized variable resolved
6. **Word count:** Email 2: 80-120 chars (short). Email 3: 50-90 words. Email 4: 80-120 words.
7. **No em dashes (—):** Use periods or commas instead
8. **Single CTA per email:** Never give multiple options to the recipient
