# ICP Guide — SMT/EMS Equipment EDM Campaigns

## What is ICP?

ICP = Ideal Customer Profile. The precise definition of who you should be 
emailing — based on job title, company type, size, geography, and triggers.

A tight ICP is the difference between a 5% reply rate and a 0.5% reply rate.

---

## Southern Machinery ICP

### Hard Filters (Must Match All)

**Job Titles (pick 2-3 for any campaign):**
- Production Manager / SMT Manager / Plant Manager
- Operations Director / Operations Manager
- Engineering Manager / Process Engineering Manager
- Procurement Manager / Sourcing Manager
- CEO / Owner / Founder (for smaller EMS factories)

**Company Type:**
- Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS)
- OEM / ODM contract manufacturers
- Vertically integrated electronics factories

**Company Size:**
- 50-5,000 employees (primary target: 100-2,000)
- Revenue hint: factories with 3-20 SMT lines

**Geography:**
- Mexico (Queretaro, Juarez, Guadalajara, Tijuana, Monterrey)
- Vietnam (Hanoi, Hai Phong, HCMC, Binh Duong)
- India (Noida, Pune, Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai)
- Europe (Poland, Romania, Hungary, Czech Republic, Serbia)
- Southeast Asia (Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines)

**Excluded:**
- Companies HQ'd in Shenzhen, Dongguan, Guangzhou, Suzhou (domestic China market)
- Competitor equipment dealers / traders
- Existing customers (suppress from campaigns)
- Companies already running Southern Machinery equipment

---

### Soft Filters (Prioritize These)

**Triggers (timing signals — usually soft, not hard):**
- Recently won new automotive/industrial customer contracts
- Announced expansion or new factory
- Equipment being purchased for first time (greenfield)
- Currently running equipment 10+ years old
- Mention of high labor turnover or labor cost issues
- Actively evaluating Japanese OEM quotes (heard from prospect directly)
- Factory moved from China to [target country] in last 3 years

**Behaviors (prioritize these leads):**
- Downloaded a competitor's ROI calculator
- Attended SMT industry trade shows (NEPCON, SMTAI, productronica)
- Mentioned specific equipment brands (Fuji, Yamaha, Panasonic) in job postings

## ICP for Different Markets

### Mexico EMS
- Focus on: automotive Tier-2 EMS, industrial electronics
- Titles: Production Manager, SMT Manager, Plant Manager
- Size: 200-2,000 employees
- Key pain: aging Japanese lines, USMCA compliance, local support
- Watch for: companies quoting Fuji/Yamaha (they're in evaluation mode)

### Vietnam EMS
- Focus on: consumer electronics, industrial, electronics transitioning from China
- Titles: Operations Manager, Production Manager, Engineering Manager
- Size: 100-1,500 employees
- Key pain: China+1 diversification, speed to market, labor cost inflation
- Watch for: factories expanding from 1 shift to 2-3 shifts

### India EMS
- Focus on: industrial electronics, automotive components, consumer electronics
- Titles: CEO/Owner, Production Manager, Procurement Manager
- Size: 50-3,000 employees
- Key pain: PLI scheme compliance, custom duties, budget approval process
- Watch for: companies with government certifications, iCAT/ISO certifications

### Europe EMS
- Focus on: industrial electronics, automotive Tier-2, medical electronics
- Titles: Operations Director, Plant Manager, Engineering Manager
- Size: 100-5,000 employees
- Key pain: energy costs, ESG requirements, compact footprint
- Watch for: companies with ISO 14001, ESG reporting requirements

## How to Build a Lead List

### Sources
1. **Prospeo** — title-first search (best for job title targeting)
2. **Apollo.io** — company + contact search (good for company data)
3. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — for verifying titles and company details
4. **Google Maps scraping** — for local factory discovery

### Minimum Lead Data Required
- First name
- Last name  
- Job title
- Company name
- Company domain (email)
- Country
- City (for local time sending)

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] No role-based emails (info@, support@, admin@, hello@)
- [ ] No personal email domains (gmail, hotmail, yahoo)
- [ ] No duplicates across the list
- [ ] Correct country/region filter applied
- [ ] Competitor domains excluded
- [ ] Existing customer domains excluded

## ICP Mistake: Being Too Broad

**Bad ICP:** "Electronics companies in Mexico"

**Good ICP:** "SMT Production Managers or Operations Directors at 
EMS factories with 200-1,500 employees in Queretaro, Juarez, or 
Guadalajara that are running Fuji or Yamaha pick-and-place equipment 
and have expanded production in the last 24 months"

The difference in reply rates is 10x.

---

## ICP Mistake: Being Too Narrow

**Bad ICP:** "VP of Operations at factories exactly 500-600 employees 
in Queretaro that make automotive electronics and use Yamaha equipment"

You'll get 50 leads instead of 5,000 and won't have statistical significance.

**Rule:** If your ICP produces fewer than 500 leads for a market, 
it's probably too narrow. Relax one criterion.
