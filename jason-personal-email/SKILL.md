## Module 1: Inbox Triage

### Triage Categories

**Category A — Hot Lead (Respond within 2 hours)**
- Specific equipment request / RFQ
- Request for quotation (RFQ)
- Visiting a trade show / expresses interest in meeting
- References a competitor quote (Fuji/Yamaha/Panasonic)
- Automotive / EV / industrial EMS context
- Signs of expansion or new factory

**Category B — Warm Lead (Respond within 24 hours)**
- General product inquiry
- Wants to know about capabilities
- Asks about delivery time
- Asks about support in specific region
- Non-specific "合作" (cooperation) language

**Category C — Nurture (Auto-reply with content, flag for follow-up)**
- Out-of-office / OOO
- Newsletter subscription confirmation
- Trade show booth confirmation
- Generic marketing inquiry

**Category D — Noise (Archive and suppress)**
- Recruitment/job application
- Competitor sales outreach

## Module 2: Customer Qualification (BANT + Extended)

Jason uses BANT + capability fit. For each inbound inquiry, assess and respond accordingly.

### Qualification Checklist

| Dimension | What to Assess | Red Flag | Green Flag |
|-----------|---------------|----------|------------|
| **Budget (B)** | Has this person seen a quote before? Are they CapEx-ready or early stage? | "We're still evaluating" without timeline | "We have budget approved for Q3" |
| **Authority (A)** | Job title — SMT Manager, Ops Director, Procurement, CEO? | Admin/assistant | Named decision-maker or influencer |
| **Need (N)** | Specific equipment pain? Legacy lines? Expansion? | Generic "looking for supplier" | Specific model numbers, pain points mentioned |
| **Timeline (T)** | When do they want equipment? | "Sometime this year" | "Proof of concept by Q2, full deployment Q3" |
| **Technical Fit** | Does their product fit SM capability? | PCBs > 510×310mm (oversize) | Within SM product range |
| **Geography** | Are they in target market? | HQ in Shenzhen/Dongguan (domestic) | Mexico, Vietnam, India, Europe, SEA |

### Qualification Response Patterns

**Qualified — Has Budget + Authority + Need + Timeline**
→ Immediately send: Product matching response + ROI model + meeting booking CTA

**Partially Qualified — Has Need but Budget/Timeline Unclear**
→ Send: Capability overview + discovery questions + set reminder for follow-up in 2 weeks

**Not Qualified — No Clear Need or Out of Scope**
→ Send: Brief + resource link + suppress from campaign

**Unqualifiable — Not Enough Info**
→ Send: Quick 3-question qualification reply + flag for manual review

## Module 4: Follow-Up Sequencing

### Standard Sequence (4-Step, Days 0-14)

| Day | Step | Subject Strategy | Goal |
|-----|------|-----------------|------|
| Day 0 | Email 1 | Problem-pain / ROI billboard | Hook + awareness |
| Day 3 | Email 2 | Threaded follow-up (no subject change) | Persistence signal |
| Day 7 | Email 3 | Case study angle (new thread) | Social proof |
| Day 14 | Email 4 | Breakup email / final offer | Urgency + unsubscribe signal |

### Follow-Up Decision Tree

```
Did contact reply to Day 0?
├── YES → Check sentiment
│   ├── Positive → Book meeting / send quote → Move to meeting tracking
│   ├── Negative → Log objection → Suppress from future campaigns
│   └── OOO → Add to re-engagement list (30 days after OOO ends)
└── NO → Send Day 3 threaded follow-up
    ├── NO reply → Day 7 case study
    │   ├── NO reply → Day 14 breakup → Then suppress for 90 days
    │   └── YES reply → Route to appropriate response path
    └── YES reply → Route to appropriate response path
```

### Key Follow-Up Rules
- **Day 3 email:** Must add new information — never resend identical email
- **Day 7 email:** Use a case study with specific numbers and geography
- **Day 14 email:** Signal you're closing the file, offer something small
- **Always BCC Jason's work email** on all outbound for oversight

## Module 7: Objection Handling

### Feel-Felt-Found Framework

**"Your equipment quality is not as good as Japanese brands"**
```
I understand that concern — it's actually the first thing most buyers ask us. 
[Feel] What we've found is that for 80% of SMT applications, the production 
output difference is imperceptible between well-engineered Chinese equipment 
and Japanese OEM lines. The gap becomes real in ultra-high-precision applications 
where sub-0.1mm accuracy matters.

[Felt] Other factories we work with — especially in Mexico and Vietnam — 
had the same hesitation initially. What changed their mind was the 30-day 
OEE performance guarantee we offer: if the machine doesn't perform, you don't pay.

[Found] What they found after 6 months was that the support response time 
was faster than what they were getting from their previous Japanese OEM, 
and the total cost of ownership was significantly lower.

Would a 30-day performance bond help address that concern?
```

**"We don't have budget right now"**
```
Totally understand — CapEx cycles can be long for capital equipment.

Here's what I'd suggest: let me put together a rough ROI model 
so you have it ready when the next budget window opens. 
Most factories find the numbers make the internal case much easier.

I can have a draft to you within 24 hours. What email should I send it to?

Best,
Jason Wu
```

**"We tried Chinese equipment before and it failed"**
```
I understand — the market has a lot of low-quality players, and 
bad experiences create lasting impressions.

The difference I'd point out is in the support infrastructure: 
we have dedicated field engineers (not contractors), 7×24 remote 
diagnostics, and we guarantee spare parts availability.

Would it help if I connected you with one of our existing customers 
in [their region] to hear about their 12-month experience firsthand?

Best,
Jason Wu
```

**"Your delivery time is too long"**
```
I want to make sure I'm comparing apples to apples: our 4-week delivery 
is for a fully configured, CE-certified machine with custom specifications.

Japanese OEM quotes for comparable equipment typically run 6-9 months. 
Even the standard Chinese suppliers I see quoted run 8-12 weeks.

If you have a tighter deadline, let's talk — I can check our current 
factory load and see if a fast-track option exists.

Best,
Jason Wu
```
