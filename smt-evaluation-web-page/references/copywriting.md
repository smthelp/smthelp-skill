# Copywriting rules

## Tone presets (page.json → tone)
- **engineering-plain** (default): short sentences, numbers first, units always, no superlatives without a number.
- **sales-warm**: same facts, opens with the customer's pain and closes each section with the benefit.
- **executive-brief**: headline = decision + number; ≤3 bullets per section; specs moved down.

## Evidence
Every claim traces to the source. Labels in slide `notes`: Verified source, Concept view (renders, drawings, comparisons), Estimate, To be confirmed (source disagrees or data missing). Recompute derived numbers; never "fix" a source number silently — show it and list the conflict in "Accuracy & open points".

## Never publish
Real customer name, people, emails, RFQ/quotation numbers, prices, payment/delivery/warranty terms, destination country, competitor brand names (use "reference machine"), certifications or yields not in the source.

## Blogs (8) — six-step order, one paragraph each
1 attention (a striking fact) · 2 pain · 3 reusable method · 4 the product/solution with numbers from this case · 5 return habit (what to check regularly) · 6 next step.
Titles and headings never contain: hook, diagnosis, framework, vehicle, loop, CTA (or 钩子/诊断/框架/载体/循环/行动号召) — build fails otherwise.

## Quick answer (GEO)
`ans_h2` = the question a buyer would type; `ans_p` = 2–3 sentences with machine, configuration and the key numbers; `qf1..6` = product, parts, machine, output, feeding/handling, basis.

## FAQ (6)
Why this method · cost in cycle time · alternative compared · operator effort · design changes needed · what you need to start.
