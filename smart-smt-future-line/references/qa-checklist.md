# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal.
- Futuristic but still practical and industrial.
- SMT/THT process is recognizable.
- One clean glow path or precision signal, not decorative neon everywhere.
- English text is short and readable.
- No fake badges, fake dashboards, or unverified numbers.
- Does not copy Xiaohei or original repository examples.

## Failure Signals

- Looks like generic AI/cybersecurity art.
- Factory is too dark to understand.
- Glow overwhelms the machine/process.
- Too many holographic screens.
- Machine details are too abstract for PCB assembly.
- Text is warped or unreadable.

## Iteration

- Too dark: increase white technical light and reduce graphite background.
- Too generic: add PCB panels, nozzles, feeders, insertion heads, AOI lens, or conveyor modules.
- Too busy: simplify to one machine and one PCB path.
- Too sci-fi: remove city/fantasy elements and return to EMS factory equipment.
