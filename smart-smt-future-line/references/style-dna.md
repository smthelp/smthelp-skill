# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Futuristic Southern Machinery production-line visuals: precise machines, glowing PCB paths, cyan motion signals, graphite depth, and short English marketing copy.

## Required

- 16:9 horizontal asset.
- Strong single focal point.
- Futuristic but practical EMS factory environment.
- Blue/graphite/cyan technical palette with controlled PCB green.
- Glowing path should show production flow or machine precision.
- English text only, 2-6 words if shown.
- Machines and PCB process must remain recognizable.
- If the Southern Machinery logo appears, use the official PNG asset rather than a generated or redrawn logo.

## Palette

- Deep machinery blue: `#0B3A75`
- Graphite: `#111827`
- Cool dark panel: `#172033`
- Cyan glow: `#17B7FF`
- PCB green: `#20A66A`
- White technical light: `#F7FAFC`

## Avoid

- Dark cyberpunk streets or sci-fi city scenes
- Random holographic UI that does not explain production
- Fake digital badges or fake dashboards
- Overexposed neon
- Crowded full-factory render
- Copying Xiaohei characters or any original example composition
- Unverified performance numbers

## Aesthetic Direction

The image should feel advanced, sharp, and market-ready, but still grounded in real PCB assembly work.
