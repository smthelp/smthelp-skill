# Smart-SMT Clean Industrial

Create clean 16:9 English marketing visuals for Southern Machinery. This version is the safest house style for B2B export marketing: white background, strong negative space, simplified machines, PCB panels, precise motion, and short English captions.

The output should feel professional, practical, and easy for EMS factory buyers to understand in three seconds.

## Read When Needed

- `references/style-dna.md`: clean industrial visual DNA, palette, and forbidden styles.
- `references/composition-patterns.md`: reusable marketing composition patterns adapted from the original repository structure.
- `references/prompt-template.md`: imagegen prompt template.
- `references/qa-checklist.md`: validation and iteration checklist.
- `assets/southern-machinery-logo-300x83.png`: official Southern Machinery logo reference asset. Use this PNG when a logo is needed; do not redraw, approximate, or replace the logo with plain text.

## Workflow

1. Identify the marketing asset: LinkedIn image, blog hero, website section, proposal cover, email visual, or product explainer.
2. Extract one business message: labor saving, line-ready automation, stable quality, high-mix EMS upgrade, complete line solution, or technical consultation.
3. Produce a short shot list first if the user asks for multiple concepts. Default to 3 concepts.
4. For generation requests, create one imagegen prompt per image. Do not combine multiple concepts into one image.
5. Keep all visible text in English. Prefer 2-6 words in-image, or no in-image text when the asset will sit beside copy.
6. Add a review note when claims involve price, delivery, warranty, certifications, custom specs, customer names, or ROI.

## Shot List Format

```markdown
## Concept 1: [Name]
Use: [where this image fits]
Message: [one focused business point]
Visual: [one-sentence image idea]
English text: "[exact text or no text]"
Imagegen prompt: [prompt]
```

```text
Automation Partner, Not Supplier
```

Use factual product categories only. Do not invent prices, lead times, certifications, client names, or ROI numbers.
