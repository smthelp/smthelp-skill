# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Clean white-space industrial illustration for Southern Machinery: simple machines, PCB flow, precise engineering, concise English, and zero clutter.

## Required

- 16:9 horizontal marketing image.
- Clean white or very light technical background.
- Simplified SMT/THT machines, conveyors, PCB panels, nozzles, feeders, AOI cameras, insertion heads, or soldering stations.
- Strong negative space: at least 35% quiet area.
- One clear business message per image.
- English text only, preferably 0-6 words inside the image.
- Crisp vector-like shapes rendered as a polished bitmap.
- Southern Machinery logo may appear small and clean when useful; use the official PNG asset, not a redrawn mark.

## Palette

- Deep machinery blue: `#0B3A75`
- Industrial graphite: `#1F2933`
- Clean white: `#F7FAFC`
- Cyan signal: `#17B7FF`
- PCB green accent: `#20A66A`
- Amber caution accent: `#F6B33D`, used sparingly

## Avoid

- Cute mascot art
- Copying Xiaohei or any specific character from the reference repository
- Busy comic panels
- Handwritten-style clutter
- Fake certification badges
- Fake prices, delivery dates, ROI numbers, or customer names
- Cyberpunk darkness
- Low-end ecommerce poster style
- Dense PPT flowcharts

## Aesthetic Direction

Make the scene feel like a modern industrial product sketch that became a polished marketing asset: simple, direct, confident, and credible.
