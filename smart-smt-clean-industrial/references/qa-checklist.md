# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal.
- One dominant focal point.
- Clear SMT/THT or PCB assembly automation subject.
- Clean white or very light background.
- Strong negative space.
- English text only, short and readable.
- No copied Xiaohei character or original-reference composition.
- No fake claims about price, delivery, certification, ROI, or customer names.
- Looks B2B industrial, not childish or decorative.

## Failure Signals

- Looks like a generic tech startup illustration.
- Looks like a PPT process diagram.
- Too many arrows, labels, modules, or UI screens.
- In-image text is long, misspelled, or too small.
- The machine/process is unclear to an EMS buyer.
- Color palette becomes all-blue without PCB green or white balance.

## Iteration

- Too generic: add specific PCB assembly details such as nozzles, feeders, insertion heads, PCB panels, or conveyors.
- Too busy: remove nodes and keep one machine/process.
- Too flat: add subtle depth while staying clean.
- Too salesy: remove badges and let the machine/process carry the message.
