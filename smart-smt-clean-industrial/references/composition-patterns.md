# Composition Patterns

Choose one structure per image.

## Machine Hero

Best for product-category visuals and website sections.

Composition: one simplified machine or machine module dominates the center; PCB panels pass through it; optional logo and short headline sit in open space.

## Process Line

Best for complete SMT + THT solution visuals.

Composition: left-to-right line with 4-6 simplified stations. Use one clean cyan path or arrow, not many arrows.

## Problem To Automation

Best for labor-saving and upgrade messages.

Composition: left side shows a simplified manual bottleneck; right side shows a cleaner automated process. Keep the comparison respectful and non-dramatic.

## Precision Close-Up

Best for technical posts and thumbnails.

Composition: close-up of a nozzle, insertion head, AOI camera, soldering wave, or LED placement. Use PCB detail as context, not a busy background.

## Engineering Support

Best for consultation and proposal CTAs.

Composition: simplified engineer beside PCB data, line plan, or sample board. The visual should say "we help design the line" without becoming a meeting cartoon.

## Originality Rule

Borrow the original repository's clarity and one-idea discipline, not its IP, linework, or specific compositions. Smart-SMT must feel like Southern Machinery's own B2B industrial system.
