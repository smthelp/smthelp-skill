# Gen Outreach

## Purpose

Create targeted outreach that feels consultative rather than generic, with channel-specific length and CTA.

## Required Inputs

- Customer company, country, contact role, industry, and estimated factory size when available.
- Product focus such as LED boards, consumer electronics EMS, automotive electronics, industrial control, radial insertion, axial insertion, odd-form insertion, or LED mounting.
- Pain point or trigger: labor cost, unstable quality, expansion, manual insertion, old equipment, new factory, or trade show.
- Preferred channel: LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, or mixed sequence.

## Workflow

1. Identify the likely pain point from the customer profile.
2. Choose one Southern Machinery capability or machine direction that fits the profile.
3. Use one verified data point only when relevant: CPH, labor replacement, one-stop line, or ROI logic.
4. Draft separate channel versions when requested.
5. End with a low-friction CTA such as asking about PCB type, monthly output, worker count, or interest in a 15-minute call.

## Output Format

```markdown
## LinkedIn Version
[150 words or fewer unless requested otherwise, consultative CTA]

## Email Version
Subject: [specific subject]

[Body under 300 words with signature]

## Personalization Notes
- Pain point used:
- Recommended direction:
- Missing data:
```

## Southern Machinery Rules

- Default customer-facing copy to English.
- Do not invent customer facts, prices, delivery times, or unverified specs.
- Keep the tone professional, concise, and solution-selling oriented.
- Use `www.smthelp.com` and `jasonwu@smthelp.com` when a contact point is useful.
- Add the Southern Machinery email signature for full email drafts.
