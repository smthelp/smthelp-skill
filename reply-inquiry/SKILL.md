# Reply Inquiry

## Purpose

Create concise, consultative replies to customer inquiries while capturing missing information for sales follow-up.

## Workflow

1. Determine the inquiry type: quotation request, catalog request, product selection, technical question, ROI question, competitor comparison, after-sales/spare parts, or unclear.
2. If enough data exists, personalize the reply around the customer pain point and recommended direction.
3. If data is missing, ask 3-5 qualification questions without making the message feel like a form.
4. Use the appropriate channel format:
   - Email: subject line + body under 300 words + signature.
   - WhatsApp: under 100 words, natural tone, no long tables.
   - LinkedIn: short consultative note and light CTA.
5. End with a clear CTA such as sharing PCB photos/BOM, monthly output, worker count, or scheduling a call.

## Email Structure

```markdown
Subject: [specific subject]

Hi [Name],

[Thank them + acknowledge requirement.]

[Short recommendation or next-step logic with verified specs only.]

To recommend the right solution, could you please share:
1. [question]
2. [question]
3. [question]

[CTA]

[Signature]
```

## Shared Southern Machinery Rules

- Default customer-facing output to English unless the user requests another language.
- Keep emails under 300 words, WhatsApp messages under 100 words, and LinkedIn posts at 150-300 words with 3 relevant hashtags.
- Use consultative selling: identify pain points, use verified data, position Southern Machinery as an automation partner, and end with a clear CTA.
- Do not invent prices, lead times, unavailable specifications, customer names, or unverified competitor claims.
- Treat product specs as catalog-based; if a requested spec is outside known data, say it needs further confirmation via `jasonwu@smthelp.com`.
- Include assumptions for ROI or capacity calculations so the customer can verify them.
- Add the Southern Machinery email signature when drafting a full email.
