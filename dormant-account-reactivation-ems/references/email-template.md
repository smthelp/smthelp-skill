# Email Outreach Template (Fill-in-the-Blank)

Use this template for any dormant EMS account reactivation. Replace all `<...>` placeholders. Target length: 350-450 words for the body. Never use filler greetings. Always lead with a verifiable fact about the customer.

---

## Subject Line Options (pick one)

1. `Re: <Customer City> Factory Expansion - <Specific Bottleneck> Strategy for <New Product Line>`
2. `<Customer Name> <City> - Solving the <THT/DIP> Bottleneck Before <New SMT Line> Goes Live`
3. `Quick Question on Your <New Product Line> THT Process - <Customer Name>`

**Subject rules:**
- Never use all caps
- Never use multiple exclamation marks
- Never start with "Free" or "Special Offer"
- Always include the customer city or company name (proves non-blast)
