# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Engineer-led Southern Machinery storytelling: simple, clean, slightly playful industrial scenes where a simplified operator solves one PCB assembly problem.

## Required

- 16:9 horizontal article or social post illustration.
- Clean white or very light background.
- One simplified engineer/operator figure participates in the core action.
- The figure is neutral and industrial: small helmet or work jacket is okay; no mascot identity.
- Simple machines, PCB panels, cables, sample boards, line plans, conveyors, or testing fixtures.
- English labels only, 0-5 short labels.
- One idea per image.
- If the Southern Machinery logo appears, use the official PNG asset rather than a generated or redrawn logo.

## Character Rules

The recurring figure is a simplified Southern Machinery engineer/operator, not Xiaohei.

Use:

- Simple human silhouette or minimal line character.
- Safety glasses, work jacket, or small tool only when useful.
- Calm, competent, understated expression.
- Action that explains the sales point: aligning, checking, connecting, sorting, testing, repairing, planning.

Avoid:

- Black blob creature
- White-dot eyes as an IP signature
- Mascot poses
- Cute cartoon exaggeration
- Complex facial expression
- Copying original Xiaohei actions or compositions

## Palette

- Black/graphite linework
- Southern Machinery blue for equipment and key structure
- Cyan for automation signal or feedback
- PCB green for boards
- Amber or red only for bottlenecks or caution points

## Aesthetic Direction

Borrow the original repository's clarity, restraint, and one-action storytelling. Replace the strange hand-drawn IP with a credible industrial sales-engineer narrative.
