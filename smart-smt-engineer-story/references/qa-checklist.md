# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal.
- One simplified engineer/operator is involved in the core action.
- The character is not Xiaohei and not a mascot.
- The action explains a real SMT/THT sales or production concept.
- English labels are short, sparse, and readable.
- Background is clean and uncluttered.
- No copied original repository composition.
- No fake commercial or technical claims.

## Failure Signals

- Character looks like a cute mascot.
- Character only stands beside the process.
- Scene becomes a comic strip with too many panels.
- It is unclear what SMT/THT problem is being solved.
- The image feels like generic office productivity rather than PCB assembly.
- Text becomes long or unreadable.

## Iteration

- Character too decorative: make the engineer align, test, bridge, inspect, or connect a process element.
- Too generic: add PCB boards, conveyors, insertion heads, nozzles, AOI lens, or machine modules.
- Too cute: simplify expression and reduce character emphasis.
- Too text-heavy: remove labels and let the action communicate.
