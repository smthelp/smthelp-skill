# Skill: Objection Handling — Global ("China Quality")

## Context
Use when a prospect raises quality, reliability, or "China manufacturing" concerns — the single most common blocker in non-China EMS markets. Applies to all regions but especially Europe, North America, and Japan-adjacent markets.

## Key Facts
- Southern Machinery CE-certified (EU machinery directive)
- ISO 9001 quality system maintained since 2011
- S-70LD runs at 98.6% UPH in Polish automotive EMS (CaseStudy: PolandAutoEMS_2023)
- 50+ full lines deployed globally
- Spare parts ship within 48h from Shenzhen warehouse
- Remote diagnostics via n8n + camera integration available

## Feel–Felt–Found Script

**Objection:** "Your quality can't match Fuji / Yamaha / Panasonic"

```
I understand your concern — that's the first question every buyer asks us, and it's the right question to ask.

Many of our current European clients felt the same way when we first approached them. They were running Fuji and Yamaha lines for 8–12 years and were skeptical of any Chinese equipment.

What they found is that our CE-certified lines passed their ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 audits in the first inspection cycle — no exceptions, no rework. The S-70LD has been running at 98.6% UPH in a Polish automotive electronics plant since 2023 with zero critical incidents.

The difference is: we're not selling you a machine. We're selling you a complete PCBA line with process recipes, installation, and ongoing support — backed by a team that's been doing this since 2011.

Would it help if I shared the audit reports from our Polish and Thai installations?
```

## Pitfalls
- Do NOT disparage Fuji/Yamaha — reference them neutrally
- Do NOT over-promise — only cite verifiable deployment data
- Do NOT say "just as good" — say "audited to the same standards"

## Verification
- Offer to share CE certificate + ISO 9001 scope
- Offer customer reference in same vertical/region
- Offer trial run on their board sample before any commitment
