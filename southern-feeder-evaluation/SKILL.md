# Southern Feeder Evaluation

Use this workflow to determine whether Southern Machinery can match a feeder to a customer's components and placement machine. Do not promise compatibility from component size alone.

## Required Inputs

Collect the component template and the target placement-machine information together.

### Component List

Use [assets/Components for MVF_SM.xlsx](assets/Components%20for%20MVF_SM.xlsx) as the customer template. Ask for one row per component with:

- Part number and clear description.
- Clear photos of the actual component, including pins and orientation where relevant.
- `PW`, `PL`, `H`, and `LH` dimensions in mm.
- Any special orientation, lead-condition, surface-sensitivity, or packaging notes.

Treat `MVF judgment` as Southern Machinery engineering output. Do not ask the customer to self-approve it.

### Target Mounter

Require:

- Placement-machine brand and exact model.
- Photos of the feeder rail, available position, and mounting/interface area when possible.
- Existing feeder type, feeder P/N, or interface reference when available.
- Required feeder cycle, component quantity, and expected production use.

Ask for component samples or detailed mechanical drawings when the photos and dimensions do not establish physical orientation or pin condition.

## Evaluation Flow

1. Restate the received component and mounter facts. List missing items only.
2. Check whether the component can be physically distinguished and presented consistently at the pick point.
3. Flag risks directly: deformable pins, undefined orientation, insufficient photos, unclear dimensions, or an unconfirmed mounter interface.
4. State that Southern Machinery engineering must match the fixture/interface and confirm the closest feeder configuration from the current catalog.
5. For a customer-facing webpage or RFQ form, add two clearly separated blocks:
   - `Complete the component template`: link the workbook and enumerate the fields.
   - `Identify the placement machine`: require the exact brand/model and interface evidence.
6. Provide a mailto or form action that asks the customer to attach the completed workbook and include the target mounter model.

## Customer-Facing Language

Use concise English unless the customer writes in another language. Use this positioning:

> A feeder is matched to both the physical component and the placement machine. Submit the completed component list and the exact target mounter model so Southern Machinery can confirm the suitable feeder configuration.

Do not claim universal compatibility, fixed output, or a final feeder model until the engineering review is complete.

## Completion Check

Before sending an evaluation request or publishing a landing-page section, confirm that:

- The workbook download links to the supplied template.
- The customer is asked for photos, part number, description, and all four dimensions.
- The target mounter brand and exact model are required.
- `MVF judgment` is described as an engineering confirmation.
- A clear next action tells the customer how to submit the package.
