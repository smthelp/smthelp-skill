# Terms library (clean English for quotation terms)

Pick a line, adjust numbers. Keep one fact per sentence. Incoterms written "EXW Shenzhen, China (Incoterms® 2020)".

## Price basis
- EXW Shenzhen, China (Incoterms® 2020), in USD
- FOB Shenzhen, China (Incoterms® 2020), in USD
- CIF <port>, <country> (Incoterms® 2020), in USD - freight and insurance to the named port included
- DAP <city>, <country> (Incoterms® 2020) - import duties and taxes paid by the buyer

## Payment terms (must add up to 100 %)
- 100% T/T before shipment
- 50% T/T deposit with order, 50% T/T before shipment
- 30% T/T deposit with order, 70% T/T before shipment
- 30% T/T deposit, 70% by irrevocable L/C at sight
- Avoid on EXW/FOB: balance "after installation" with no day cap. If the customer insists: "50% deposit, 40% before shipment, 10% within 30 days after installation or 60 days after shipment, whichever is earlier".

## Delivery time (always give a start point)
- Within 30 days after receipt of the deposit
- Within 4 weeks after receipt of full payment
- Ex stock, ships within 5 working days after receipt of payment (only if Jason confirms stock)

## Warranty (period + start + exclusions)
- 12 months from the date of shipment, excluding consumable and wear parts and damage caused by misuse
- 12 months from installation acceptance, or 14 months from shipment, whichever comes first
- Lifetime remote technical support by email, WhatsApp and video call

## Installation & training (say remote or on-site, and who pays travel)
- Free remote installation guidance and operator training by video call
- Our service engineer's international air fare, on-site installation and training are free of charge. The buyer covers the engineer's local transport, accommodation and meals during the visit.
- On-site installation and training: USD <x> per engineer-day plus travel, if required

## Packing
- Export packing: vacuum-sealed, fumigation-free wooden case (ISPM 15 compliant)
- Export packing as listed above. Freight from Shenzhen is arranged and paid by the buyer (EXW).

## Insurance
- To be covered by the buyer
- Included to the named port (CIF/CIP)

## Taxes & other
- Prices exclude import duties, local taxes and bank charges in the buyer's country
- Any terms not stated here follow the sales contract
- Photos are for reference; the machine supplied follows the specifications above.

## Spec line style
- "Label: value unit", units spaced (430 mm, 5.2 kW, 4 - 6 kgf/cm²), ranges "0.6 - 2.0 mm", "L x W x H", ASCII "x" not "*", no full-width punctuation.
