# Trade Show Leads

## Purpose

Create event-specific outreach and follow-up workflows for trade-show prospects.

## Workflow

1. Capture event name, booth number, date, customer name, company, country, interest model, and meeting status.
2. Segment the lead: pre-show appointment, booth visitor follow-up, catalog-only visitor, technical meeting, or quotation request.
3. Draft concise messages with event context and a clear next step.
4. For batch outputs, create one message per company and keep file-safe names if saving files.
5. After the show, recommend lead grade and next action based on specificity of interest.

## Message Templates

### Pre-show Appointment Email
Use subject: `[Event] – Meet Southern Machinery at Booth [Booth]`.
Invite the customer to a 15-minute meeting and mention the machine category they care about.

### Post-show Follow-up
Thank the customer for visiting, recap their interest, ask for missing technical details, and offer a call or proposal.

## Shared Southern Machinery Rules

- Default customer-facing output to English unless the user requests another language.
- Keep emails under 300 words, WhatsApp messages under 100 words, and LinkedIn posts at 150-300 words with 3 relevant hashtags.
- Use consultative selling: identify pain points, use verified data, position Southern Machinery as an automation partner, and end with a clear CTA.
- Do not invent prices, lead times, unavailable specifications, customer names, or unverified competitor claims.
- Treat product specs as catalog-based; if a requested spec is outside known data, say it needs further confirmation via `jasonwu@smthelp.com`.
- Include assumptions for ROI or capacity calculations so the customer can verify them.
- Add the Southern Machinery email signature when drafting a full email.

## Core Product Facts

- `S3010A`: radial component auto insertion, `12,000 CPH`, replaces `8-10` workers.
- `S4000`: axial component auto insertion, `13,000 CPH`, replaces `10` workers.
