3. **S3000 high-speed radial inserter** (30,000 CPH)

   - Matches ISO 50001 low-energy mandate (energy focus is at the corporate level)

5. **S-1688 / SME-6200 PCBA cleaning machine**
   - Aqueous in-line cleaning + stencil cleaning
   - Post-clean ionic contamination: <0.5 μg NaCl/cm²
   - Critical for: inverter motor driver reliability, eliminates no-clean residue field failures
