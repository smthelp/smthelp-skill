## 产品核心定位

**一句话卖点**：The S-680A helps SMT factories automate scrap carrier tape cutting, reduce manual labor, improve 5S, and manage waste tape more efficiently — at a cost-effective Shenzhen manufacturing price.

**三大价值锚点**：
1. **Labor Savings** — 减少操作员手动剪带工时，释放到更高价值的 Pick and Place 监控任务
2. **5S & ESG Compliance** — 废料体积减少90%，通过 ISO 14001 / ESG 审核
3. **High Cost-Performance** — 深圳制造，价格比日本/欧美同类低40-50%，4周交期

**技术参数速查**：
- Cutting length: 5–150mm (PLC-adjustable)
- Speed: 76 mm/sec
- Blade: Rotational gear blade, 4×寿命 vs 普通刀片
- Power: 220VAC 50/60Hz, 100W
- Dimensions: 900×220×400mm, ≈65kg
- Tape width: 标准8–56mm carrier tape（纸带 & embossed塑料带）
- Noise: <70dB
- Control: PLC-driven automation

**不夸大原则**：S-680A 不直接提升 CPH，但通过减少人工干预和废料堆积，间接保护 Pick and Place 线速稳定性和 Feeder/Nozzle 清洁度。

## 7. SPIN 销售话术卡

### 4步SPIN框架

**S — Situation（了解现状）**：
- "How many Pick and Place lines do you currently operate?"
- "What is your approximate daily consumption of SMD reels?"
- "How is scrap carrier tape currently handled — manual cutting, old cutter, or direct disposal?"

**P — Problem（暴露痛点）**：
- "Have you noticed any impact on operator efficiency from manual tape cutting?"
- "Does scrap tape accumulation ever cause 5S audit issues or floor safety concerns?"
- "Have you seen any Feeder jams or AOI false calls that might be related to loose tape debris?"

**I — Implication（放大影响）**：
- "If operators spend 2–3 hours per shift on manual waste handling, what's the opportunity cost? Could they be doing SPI/AOI inspection instead?"
- "If the Pick and Place area is cluttered with loose tape, how does that affect your first-pass yield or CPH consistency?"
- "With rising labor costs in [Region], how will your factory maintain margins if manual processes aren't automated?"

**N — Need-Payoff（引导成交）**：
- "If we could eliminate manual tape cutting and free up 2 operator hours per shift, how would you redeploy that time?"
- "If your next ESG audit showed a 90% reduction in waste volume, what would that mean for your sustainability score?"
- "If the S-680A paid for itself in under 6 months, would that justify moving forward this quarter?"
