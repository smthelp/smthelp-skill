## 模板 B：英文客户回复邮件

```
Subject: Re: [原主题] — [方案关键词] from Southern Machinery SMTHELP

Dear [客户名],

Thank you for reaching out to Southern Machinery SMTHELP. 

**Understanding your need**
- [一句话确认理解客户需求]
- [行业/产品/工艺方向]

**Recommended direction**
[3-5句描述推荐方向，聚焦工艺匹配度和为什么合适，不报型号不报价]

**Why this fits**
- [卖点1：OEE/人工/良率/换线/ROI]
- [卖点2]

**[CTA — 以下三选一，只能出现一条]**

Best regards,
[销售名]
Southern Machinery SMTHELP
```

### CTA 三选一

| CTA | 适用场景 | 邮件中的措辞示例 |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| **Book a line assessment** | 现有产线、升级/瓶颈痛点 | "Would you be available for a brief call or line assessment? We can review your current setup and identify the most impactful upgrade path." |
| **Send BOM and PCB files** | 缺工艺细节 | "To provide a more precise recommendation, could you share a sample BOM and PCB Gerber file? This will help us match the exact feeder configuration and placement strategy." |
| **Request turnkey proposal** | 整线新建、大客户 | "Based on your target output, we would like to prepare a turnkey proposal with full line configuration, layout, and ROI analysis. Could you confirm your preferred timeline for commissioning?" |

## 卖点锚定速查

| 痛点信号 | 主卖点 | 话术方向 |
|---------|--------|---------|
| 客户提 bottleneck/停机/产能不足 | **OEE** | 提升稼动率、减少非计划停机 |
| 客户提 labor cost/招工难 | **人工** | 自动化替代人工、减员增效 |
| 客户提 yield/defect/返修 | **良率** | SPI+AOI闭环、工艺可控 |
| 客户提 changeover/多品种小批量 | **换线** | 快速换线、程序秒切 |
| 客户提 budget/payback/investment | **ROI** | 12-18个月回本、省30-50% |
| 客户看重软件/数据/集成 | **智能** | ERP/MES集成、开放API、数据驱动 |
| 客户提 simple/no automation | **性价比** | 保留核心品质、去掉非必要功能降本 |
| 航空航天/医疗认证客户 | **合规** | 原厂OE、认证追溯、零风险 |
