## Body (250-320 words, English B2B professional)

## Tone rules

- **Sentence 1 of the body** must do 2 things in 8-15 words: (1) re-establish the relationship, (2) anchor it to a specific PO
- **NEVER** start with "I hope this email finds you well" alone — that's AI-tell
- **NEVER** use "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "industry-leading"
- **Use specific numbers** (€400,000/year, 10-15 years, 30-50% CapEx, 14-month payback) — engineers buy on data
- **Address the customer by first name** in the opening and close — not "Dear Sir/Madam"
- **Sign-off P.S.** always names a 2nd contact (HR or Procurement) — this is the highest-converting move for EU customers
