## DISCOVERY CALL QUESTIONS (Use After Reply / Meeting)

### PCB & Product Specs
1. "What is your minimum component size — are you running 01005, 0402, or larger?"
2. "What is your maximum PCB dimensions — and what is your smallest batch size per run?"
3. "Monthly volume — total boards, and how many unique SKUs per month?"
4. "What percentage of your components are through-hole — connectors, relays, transformers?"

### Current Equipment & Pain
5. "Which machine on your line is the current bottleneck — printer, P&P, or reflow?"
6. "What is your current defect rate by type — tombstoning, bridging, void, cold joint?"
7. "How many job changeovers do you run per shift, and what does changeover cost you in time?"
8. "What are your operators' main frustrations with the current line?"

### Compliance & Quality
9. "Which OEM customer audits are coming up — automotive, medical, industrial?"
10. "Are you currently running lead-free (SAC305) or SnPb? And do you measure void rate?"
11. "What solder alloy does your wave solder use — and have you measured hole-fill recently?"

### Business & Decision
12. "What is your expansion timeline — are you capacity-constrained right now?"
13. "Who else should be in the room when we discuss the financial case?"
14. "Have you looked at any other suppliers, and what are they quoting you?"
