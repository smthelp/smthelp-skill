# SPIN Tactic Templates — CEO Pipeline Report
# Reusable SPIN question trees for Section C Monday Priority Targets

## Tactic 1: Commercial Breakthrough — CFO/CEO Transition Account

Use when: New CEO or CFO appointed in last 6 months. Creates a 90-day vendor re-evaluation window.

### SPIN Sequence

**SITUATION**:
Reference the specific appointment date.
"We understand [Company] recently underwent C-suite transition — [Name] appointed as [Title] on [Date]. C-suite transitions typically trigger vendor performance reviews at the 90-day mark."

**PROBLEM**:
Name the specific cost drain from historical vendor relationship.
"Historical procurement of [old vendor, e.g., UIC spare parts] from [year-year] is consuming approximately [EUR/USD XK-YK] annually in [12-week lead time charges / premium pricing]. Your Poland/China/Mexico factory comes online [date] — supply chain disruption is not acceptable at that scale."

**IMPLICATION**:
Connect 3-factory expansion to 3x budget scrutiny.
"With [N] factories now ([Country A] + [Country B] + [Country C]), your THT line CapEx faces [Nx] the scrutiny. A [N]-month payback on Southern Machinery equipment solves the cost-per-operator equation that the new CFO will demand before approving any capex."

**NEED-PAYOFF**:
Low-friction ask with fast response promise.
"Submit your current [old vendor] feeder model list — we reply within 4 hours with cross-compatible feeder pricing and [2]-week delivery from Shenzhen. No engineering change required on your end."

### Channel
- Primary: Email to Purchasing + Technical contacts simultaneously
- Follow-up: WhatsApp to Technical contact in 48 hours

## Tactic 2: Technical + Commercial Decision — Post-Factory-Visit Stall

Use when: Factory visit completed, quality confirmed, but no PO issued. Buyer is stalling on budget or shift scheduling.

### SPIN Sequence

**PROBLEM AGITATION**:
Confirm quality, pivot to scheduling bottleneck.
"[Contact Name] — your factory visit confirmed our [machine model] placement quality meets your [IPC class / PPM] requirements. Now the real question: shift scheduling. Your current line is running at what CPH? If below [threshold, e.g., 14,000] effective, our [S6600回流焊 model] pre-heat calibration can recover [8-12%] thermal efficiency — that is real takt time improvement."

**BUDGET JUSTIFICATION**:
ROI in their local currency/labor context.
"At [USD X] + [USD Y], against your current labor cost structure in [country], our ROI model shows [11-13] month payback. What financing terms does your controller need to get this into the Q[next quarter] capex cycle?"

**URGENCY LEVER**:
Production slot scarcity.
"Our production slot for [next month] delivery closes [day after tomorrow]. We can guarantee [month after] startup if PO confirmed by [specific day]."

### Channel
- Primary: WhatsApp direct to buyer-level contact
- Offer: Free remote FAT (factory acceptance test) video call before shipment

## Tactic 3: Technical Spec Clarity — Quotation Blocked by Missing Parameters

Use when: Formal quotation cannot be issued because client has not provided bare board dimensions, CPH targets, N2 purity, JEDEC tray dimensions, or feeder model numbers.

### SPIN Sequence

**IMPLICATION**:
Connect missing spec to regulatory or operational risk.
"[Contact Name] — you need [CE/IPC/JEDEC] certification for the [machine type] to pass [Russian regulatory inspection / customer quality audit]. Without confirmed [specific missing parameter, e.g., N2 purity and JEDEC tray dimensions], we cannot issue a [CE-compliant] quotation. Every week of delay pushes your [MSD component moisture sensitivity / production launch] risk higher."

**NEED-PAYOFF**:
Trade: 3 fields of data for formal quotation within 24 hours.
"Send us: (1) [missing field 1], (2) [missing field 2], (3) [missing field 3]. In return: we issue formal [CE-compliant] quotation within 24 hours with [IEC 61010-1 test report / pre-filled layout proposal] included."

### Channel
- Primary: Email with technical data request form attached
- Follow-up: WhatsApp within 48 hours with voice note explaining the 3-field form

## SPIN Quick Reference Table

| Stage | What It Does | Typical Trigger Phrase |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|
| SITUATION | Establishes facts both parties agree on | "I understand you recently..." |
| PROBLEM | Names the cost or efficiency drain | "Your current setup is consuming..." |
| IMPLICATION | Projects pain into future/scale | "If this continues, by [time]..." |
| NEED-PAYOFF | Client articulates their own solution | "If we could reduce that by X..." |

## Hard Rules

- Never skip from SITUATION directly to NEED-PAYOFF — the IMPLIED intermediate steps are what make SPIN work.
- Always quantify the problem in local currency or local labor cost — never use default USD assumptions without stating them.
- NEED-PAYOFF must come from the client's mouth (or be phrased as a leading question they will naturally agree to). Do not claim the outcome for them.
- Channel matters: Problem Agitation + Urgency Lever tactics work best on WhatsApp (intimate, fast). Technical spec tactics work best via Email (formal, documented).
