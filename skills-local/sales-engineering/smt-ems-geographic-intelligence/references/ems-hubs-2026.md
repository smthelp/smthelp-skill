- Geographic proximity: Same-day logistics to US automotive OEM plants in Texas/Detroit

**Target buyer archetypes for ABM:**

2. **Home appliance ODM** (Flex, Jabil Guadalajara): High-volume white goods PCBs, cost-driven, fast changeover critical
3. **Industrial electronics EMS** (Scanfil, Creation): Medium-mix, industrial power supplies, longer sales cycles

## Brazil Electronics Manufacturing

### Primary Cluster: São Paulo / Campinas

**Key players (2025–2026):**

| Company | SP Location | Products | Expansion Signal |
|---------|------------|---------|----------------|
| Evolute | Manaus | Industrial PCB, LED drivers | R$80M SMT investment announced 2024 |
| Flex | Sorocaba, Hortolandia | Automotive modules, consumer | Growing nearshoring interest |
| Jabil | Campinas region | Electronics manufacturing | Evaluating Brazil expansion |
| Foxconn | Brazil operations | Consumer electronics, components | Stable base operations |
| Siemens | São Paulo | Industrial automation PCB | New energy efficiency product line |
| Bosch | Sorocaba | Automotive components, BCM | Local automotive demand strong |

**Brazil-specific drivers:**
- **Nova Indústria Brasil (NIB)**: Government policy launched 2024 targeting 20% industrial output growth by 2030; electronics = priority sector; subsidies and tax incentives for local PCB assembly
- **Wage levels**: R$2,800–R$4,500/month industrial operators (≈ $4.00–6.00/hr USD at PPP); rising ~10–15%/yr
- **USD/BRL**: Favorable exchange rate making Brazilian manufacturing competitive for export
- **Automotive cluster**: Stellantis (formerly Fiat), Volkswagen, GM all operate major SP plants; local Tier-2 suppliers under pressure to localize ECU/module assembly
- **Home appliance manufacturing**: Brastemp, Consul, Electrolux all operate in SP; control board production represents THT opportunity

**Target buyer archetypes:**
1. **Automotive Tier-2 suppliers**: BCM, ECU module makers for Stellantis/GM/VW — quality-driven, IPC-610 Class 3 required
2. **White goods EMS**: Brastemp/Consul control board suppliers — cost-driven, high volume
3. **Industrial electronics**: Power supply, LED driver manufacturers — medium-mix, moderate complexity
