Use this template for the secondary touch (after email, or as standalone for warmer accounts). Target length: 80-120 words. Always bilingual: local language for the emotional hook, English for the technical terms (engineers understand English tech terms universally).

## WhatsApp Hard Rules

- **Word count:** 80-120 words MAX. WhatsApp users have 3-second attention.
- **No attachments:** WhatsApp delivery of PDFs gets ignored. Direct them to URL instead.
- **Local language opener:** ALWAYS open in the local language, even for one sentence. Signals "I'm not a bot."
- **English tech terms:** SMT, THT, CPH, Feeder, Nozzle, Pick and Place, Reflow Oven, Wave Soldering, AOI, SPI, MES -- always in English, even within non-English sentences. Engineers understand them.
- **One CTA only:** WhatsApp needs exactly ONE low-friction action. Multiple CTAs cause decision paralysis.
- **URL always at end:** www.SMThelp.com -- the catalog is the bait, not the conversation.
- **No "?" for "Free demo":** Frame as a study/audit, not a sales demo. Lower psychological barrier.
- **No emoji in B2B:** Engineers and plant managers find emoji unprofessional.

## Follow-Up Sequence (after no reply to first WhatsApp)

**Day 3 follow-up (if no reply):**
Send a relevant case study image (uploaded to https://ph.smthelp.com first, then share URL). Example: a photo of a real Southern Machinery S70LD line in operation at a similar factory. One-line caption: "This is what we just delivered in <comparable country>. Same components you described."

**Day 7 follow-up (if still no reply):**
Drop the pitch entirely. Share a market insight: "Brazil's new ZFM local content rule takes effect in 6 months. Most EMS factories in Manaus have not yet started automation planning. We are helping 3 of them right now -- happy to share the playbook if useful."

**Day 14 follow-up (final touch):**
Direct value offer: "I will not keep messaging. If you ever want a no-cost ROI study for your DIP line, my direct WhatsApp is <number>. The offer is open for 6 months."

**After Day 14:** Move to quarterly nurture only (LinkedIn share + email newsletter). Do NOT keep pushing.
