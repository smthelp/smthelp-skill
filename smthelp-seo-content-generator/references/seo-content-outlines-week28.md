#### I. HOOK — The Soldering Choice That Costs $50K Per Year (150-200 words)
Every EMS factory with a DIP line faces the same fork: traditional wave soldering or selective soldering? The decision is rarely made on pure engineering merit — it's usually made by default ("we've always used wave") or by vendor influence. But when you run the numbers, the choice between wave and selective焊接 can mean the difference between **$30K and $80K in annual soldering consumables** alone — not counting defects, rework, and throughput. #### II. PROBLEM DEFINITION — When Standard Wave Solder Falls Short (300-400 words)
Traditional wave soldering is highly efficient for through-hole boards with relatively uniform thermal profiles — but modern B2B EMS products increasingly mix requirements that challenge the standard wave:

- **Mixed technology boards:** THT + SMT on same board, where SMT components near wave zones create thermal risk
- **High-mass connectors:** Large ground pins absorb heat, causing cold solder joints if contact time isn't sufficient
- **Fine-pitch components:** 0.5mm and below can trap solder and create bridges
- **RoHS lead-free transition:** SAC305 requires higher preheat (150-180°C) and peak (250-265°C), stressing thermal budgets

**Industry yield benchmarks:**
- Standard wave soldering: **98.5-99.2% first-pass yield** (well-tuned)
- Selective soldering: **99.4-99.7% first-pass yield**
- Rework cost per defect: **$15-$45 per joint** (labor + consumables + inspection)
- Annual rework budget for a 500K-unit/year DIP line: **$50,000-$200,000** depending on defect density

The real question isn't "which is better" — it's "which is better for YOUR board mix."

**Quantified Impact:**
