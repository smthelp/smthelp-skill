# Wa Reply

## Purpose

Write concise WhatsApp messages that move the conversation to the next practical step.

## Workflow

1. Identify the message goal: answer, qualify, recommend, follow up, schedule, or send resource.
2. Keep the message under 100 words unless the user requests a longer version.
3. Use a natural tone with one clear CTA.
4. Ask no more than 3 questions in a single WhatsApp message.
5. Avoid long tables, dense specs, and formal email signatures.

## Common CTAs

- Ask for PCB photo/video, BOM, component type, board size, monthly output, worker count, or target automation goal.
- Offer a quick call or video meeting.
- Ask whether the customer wants a catalog, machine recommendation, or ROI estimate.

## Output Format

```markdown
## WhatsApp Reply
[message]

## Intent
- Goal:
- Missing info:
- Suggested next action:
```

## Southern Machinery Rules

- Use English by default unless another language is requested.
- Do not promise price, stock, or delivery time unless provided by the user.
- Keep technical claims catalog-backed.
