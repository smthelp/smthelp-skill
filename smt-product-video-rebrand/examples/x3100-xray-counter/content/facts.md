# Approved facts - Smart EMS X3100 X-ray SMD counter (Oct 2026)
Source: Southern Machinery X3100 landing page + ROI page (file.autoinsertion.com), X3100 brochure.

## Confirmed
- Closed X-ray tube rated 80 kV, limited to 50 kV; 0.01-0.089 mA
- 30 µm spatial resolution; 427 x 427 mm flat-panel detector, 3072 x 3072 px
- Up to 4 reels per X-ray shot; offline about 6-10 s per 4 reels (0201 chips); inline about 9-15 s per reel
- Counts parts down to 01005; reels 4-17 inch, max ~430 mm diameter, 92 mm high
- Reels, tubes, bags and loose parts
- 20 MP camera reads barcodes; MES / ERP / WMS integration; daily TXT / CSV / XLS records; built-in label printer
- X-ray inspection mode shows hidden defects such as broken wires
- Cabinet radiation test value < 1 µSv/h
- Offline and Inline versions; Inline = automatic loading, counting, labeling, unloading
- Remote installation and training in English; Southern Machinery, Shenzhen, since 2011; price / lead time quoted per order
