## 常见错误写法 → 正确写法
- `Co.,Limited,China` → `Co., Limited`
- `Hongkong` → `Hong Kong`
- `NO DANGEROUS.` → `Non-dangerous goods.`
- `RawMaterial` → `Raw material`
- `(Blk)` → `black`
- `60MMX50M` → `60 mm × 50 m`
- `Terms of Trade: EXW` → `Delivery terms: EXW Shenzhen, China (Incoterms® 2020)`
- `BY T/T 100% pay advance` → `100% T/T in advance`
