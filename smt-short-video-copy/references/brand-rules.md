# Brand, evidence and terminology rules

Inherits smt-brand-kit `references/brand-guide.md` and `references/feedback-log.md`; those win on conflict.

## Naming
- Maker: Shenzhen Southern Machinery Sales and Service Co., Ltd ("Southern Machinery").
- SM model only (e.g. S-JVF02). Supplier name, logo, domain, contacts and supplier model code never appear.
- Third-party machine brands (JUKI, RS-1, FUJI, Panasonic …) only to state compatibility, kept verbatim, plus the non-affiliation line in YouTube/LinkedIn descriptions.

## Never publish
price, discount, quotation numbers, lead time (incl. spare-part lead time), stock, warranty, payment terms, customer names/orders/sites,
certificates without a certificate on file, saving / yield / ROI / throw-rate percentages, OEM affiliation, competitor names.
"Free installation", "7×24 support" and similar service promises from a landing page are left out of video copy unless Jason confirms them.

## Evidence tags
- On screen: only values in `META.facts`, with unit and model, conditions kept.
- Calculated values say so: "≤ 0.63 A (15 W ÷ 24 V, calculated)".
- Unknown thresholds in the checklist: 【推测】 / [Assumed] + "confirm with engineering".
- Not documented → not claimed (video) / "if fitted … else NA" (checklist).

## Banned in headings, titles and labels
hook, diagnosis, framework, vehicle, loop, CTA — 钩子, 诊断, 框架, 载体, 循环, 行动号召.
