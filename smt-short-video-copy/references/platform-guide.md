# Platform guide

| Platform | Tone | Format | Title / text rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | Engineer voice, spec-first, searchable | 9:16, ≤ 60 s, burned subtitles + upload SRT | Title ≤ 70 chars, head keyword first, ends `#Shorts`; description: keywords in the first 2 lines, then contact line, then non-affiliation line |
| LinkedIn | Professional, problem-first, method | 9:16 or 4:5 crop; autoplays muted → burned subtitles mandatory | 3 short paragraphs: problem → method → product; end with one question; 3–5 hashtags |
| TikTok / Instagram Reels | Fast, casual, one payoff | 9:16, 15–30 s best, motion in the first second | One line + one emoji; 5–7 hashtags mixing broad (#SMT) and long-tail (#StickFeeder) + #fyp |

## SEO keyword pattern
1 head term (exact product phrase buyers type, e.g. "JUKI RS-1 vibration stick feeder") + 2–4 long-tail (problem or task: "stick feeder jam", "how to install JUKI RS-1 feeder", "SMT feeder factory acceptance test").
Audio/podcast transcripts and competitor pages are good sources for the phrases buyers use; never copy competitor names.
