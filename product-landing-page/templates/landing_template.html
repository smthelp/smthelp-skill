Buyer FAQ

What EMS engineers usually ask

Direct answers from our datasheet and operating manual. Need something more specific? Reply to this page or email jasonwu@smthelp.com.

Which tapes can the S-680A cut?
Paper carrier tape 8 – 32 mm wide and embossed plastic tape, in thicknesses from 0.05 to 1.5 mm. Very thin vinyl tape is not recommended — send us a sample for free trial cutting before ordering.
How is cutting length adjusted?
Three preset cutting zones can be selected individually or combined to give 6 effective length settings in roughly the 5 – 150 mm range. Power off → on → press [SET] → use ↑/↓ to choose zone(s) → press [ON] at AUTO to resume.
What happens if the machine jams?
The machine attempts an auto-reverse to clear the jam. If unsuccessful it stops and sounds a buzzer. The operator hits Emergency Stop, jogs the blade away from the jam with the directional buttons, clears the material manually, and resumes AUTO.
Does it work with my Yamaha / FUJI / JUKI / Universal mounter?
Yes — the S-680A is brand-agnostic. It sits next to the mounter and cuts the scrap tape coming off the back. There is no electrical or PLC integration with the mounter required.
What is the typical ROI / payback?
In most labor markets, paying back the machine in under 6 months from labor savings alone is realistic. Actual ROI depends on local wage, the number of SMT lines it services, and shift count. We can build a transparent ROI model with your numbers — request it via the quote form.
What maintenance does it need?
Empty the trash bin as required, replace the blade as required, check the helical gear every 3 months, lubricate the LM shaft as required, and check the belt tension monthly (adjust as required). The operating manual includes step-by-step procedures and part numbers.
What's the warranty & lead time?
12-month warranty from purchase date for non-human-error issues. Lead time depends on order size and stock — confirmed in writing on the formal quotation. Spare parts dispatched in 1 working day from Shenzhen.
