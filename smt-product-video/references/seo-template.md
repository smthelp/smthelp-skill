# YouTube SEO template

## Title (40–70 characters where possible)

Pattern: `<Product name> | <key variants / use case> | Southern Machinery`

Examples:
- `S7000 Odd Form Insertion Machine | Automated THT Insertion for PIN, Terminal & Tact Switch | Southern Machinery`
- `Radial Splicing Tape | AI 3/5/6 Hole Splice Tape & Cutter for Radial Auto Insertion | Southern Machinery`

## Link rules (non-negotiable)

- **Verify every link before sending.** Open each URL and confirm it loads and points at the right product page — not a similar-named one. Twice a description went out with an empty "Learn more:" / "Product page:" line; once a cutter link was missing until an audit caught it.
- Use the landing-page URL (`file.autoinsertion.com/public/Product landing Page/<file>.html`) as the canonical product link, plus the store page (`smthelp.com/product/...`) and any tool/accessory page.
- Never put a bare domain where a product link belongs. Never leave a label with no URL after it.

## Tags (13–15)

Mix: product name, model code, process terms (radial insertion, THT, PCB assembly), buyer terms (EMS, SMT, auto insertion), brand (Southern Machinery, SMThelp). Keep them as the buyer would search.

## Upload settings

- Language: English. Category: Science & Technology. Visibility: Public (only after the user confirms).
- Thumbnail: the 1280×720 file from this job, uploaded with the video.
- Upload the separate SRT as the video's English captions as well as the burned-in subtitles.
