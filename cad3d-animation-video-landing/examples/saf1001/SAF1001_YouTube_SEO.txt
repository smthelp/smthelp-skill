TITLE (68 chars):
How an Axial Tape Feeder Works | SAF1001 Lead Cut & U-Form (4K 3D)

DESCRIPTION:
Axial resistors, diodes and jump wires still arrive on paper tape, and picking them by hand is the hidden bottleneck on a mixed-technology PCB line: slow, inconsistent and easy to get wrong. The SAF1001 axial tape feeder from Southern Machinery turns a taped strip into a trimmed, U-formed component at a fixed pick point, ready for your odd-form inserter or robot.

This 4K animation is built from the feeder's own 3D CAD model and walks through every step:
• Load: a horizontal tape tray, guide blocks, press blocks and anti-reverse claws keep the strip flat and moving one way
• Index: a compact air cylinder pushes a slide with index pawls on the leads, one pitch per cycle; an adjustable stop block sets the stroke
• Trim + form: a Ø32 mm cylinder drives the moving knife up, trimming both leads against the fixed knife and bending them into a U in one stroke
• Pick point: optical fibre sensing plus air-cylinder fine-tuning, 0.8 s per feed at ±0.3 mm feeding accuracy
• Waste tape: a second cylinder chops the empty paper tape; front-end vertical drop (reflow type optional)
• Inside: solenoid valves and a PLC-based control board; key-located mounting with a spring-loaded press block
• 24 V, 0.4–0.6 MPa air, IO over an 8-core aviation plug, or standalone button operation
• Components 3×3 to 6×20 mm on 35 / 64 / 92 mm tape (customised widths on request)
• Every unit passes 1000 consecutive feeds and a 48-hour ageing test

Already installed on Panasonic, JUKI, Yamaha and Mirae odd-form machines.
