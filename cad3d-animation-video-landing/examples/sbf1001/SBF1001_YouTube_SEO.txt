TITLE:
How a Vibratory Bowl Feeder Works | SBF1001 Bulk Parts Feeder for SMT & THT (4K 3D)

DESCRIPTION:
Bulk parts arrive in a bag, but your mounter, odd-form inserter or robot cell needs them one at a time, the same way up, every cycle. The SBF1001 vibratory bowl feeder from Southern Machinery turns a bin of loose parts into an oriented stream at a fixed pick point.

This 4K animation is built from the feeder's own 3D CAD model and walks through every module:
• Coil drive under the bowl: controlled vibration, speed gears set by voltage and frequency, for parts from 3 x 3 to 50 x 50 mm and 10 g to 300 g
• Helical sorting track: the track geometry is the filter; wrong-way parts fall back and try again (direction and polarity screening are options per material)
• Separate linear vibrator + air blowing: direct vibration carries the part, air speeds it along and keeps the rail clean
• Optical fibre sensing at the pick point: 0.8 s per feed at +/-0.5 mm feeding precision
• Bowl on isolators over a height-adjustable plate; controller inside a mobile cart with casters and levelling feet; this build adds a camera and bar light on adjustable clamps
• 220 V, 0.4-0.6 MPa plant air, IO over an 8-core aviation plug, or standalone on its own buttons
• Every unit passes a 2-hour continuous feeding test and a 48-hour ageing run
