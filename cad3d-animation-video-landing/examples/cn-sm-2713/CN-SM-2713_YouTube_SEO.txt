TITLE (65 chars):
JUKI Coil Gripper Nozzle CN-SM-2713 | 3-Point Grip, No Bent Leads

DESCRIPTION:
Heavy, round coils slip and tilt on vacuum nozzles. The CN-SM-2713 custom gripper nozzle for JUKI odd-form insertion machines holds an Ø8.5 mm wire-wound coil with a self-centering 3-point grip and inserts it with straight, untouched leads.

Built from the production CAD model, this 4K animation shows exactly how it works:
• JUKI-standard nozzle shank: plug & play on the standard nozzle changer, no adapters, no head modifications
• Spring-closed toggle linkage: one plunger drives one center pin that swings twin pivot jaws open; the return spring snaps them closed
• 3-point self-centering grip: two contact pins on one jaw, one on the other
• Z-datum center stop pin: fixed insertion depth, force goes through the body, never the leads
• 21 precision parts with E-clip-retained pivot pins: rigid in production, easy to service
