The Line-Side Reality

How boards actually get damaged between machines

Placement to reflow is only two meters — but every unsupported hand-carry in between is a scratch, a dropped panel or an ESD event waiting to happen.

Hand-carrying PCBs

One board per operator per trip between loader and line. Fifty boards means fifty trips, fifty touch points, fifty chances to drop a finished panel.

Static at the worst moment

Ordinary plastic trays and bins insulate and generate charge — exactly what you do not want around populated boards leaving placement.

Wrong pitch, jammed loader

Fixed-pitch magazines that do not match the board width cause stuck extractions and bent edges at the loader elevator.

Melted racks by the oven

Standard PP magazines deform near reflow output or in warm cleaning processes — warping racks mean warped boards.

Mixed panels, mixed racks

Product changeovers leave no way to re-slot a magazine quickly, so lines run with half-empty racks and double handling.

Audit findings

ESD audits check the path boards travel. Non-dissipative containers between machines are a documented non-conformance.

FAQ

Before you order

Which type fits my process temperature?

Bench and standard transfer duty: 80 °C grade. Warm cleaning or enclosed processes: 120 °C. Reflow-adjacent, curing ovens or sustained heat: 200 °C grade polymer, or Type D full aluminum where heat exposure is constant. Tell us the temperature at the point of contact and we pick the material.

Will it fit my loader / unloader?

The series matches standard magazine loader and unloader elevators — send your machine model and we confirm chassis compatibility. 3D drawings are downloadable above for CAD checks before you commit.

Clip or screw adjustment — which should I choose?

Clips re-pitch in seconds with no tools, ideal for frequent changeovers. Screws lock pitch permanently — better for high vibration, high cycle count or audited repeatability. Both are available on most chassis sizes.

What PCB widths can one rack carry?

Slot pitch adjusts across roughly 50–250 mm, so one rack serves multiple panel families. Chassis sizes from 355 to 630 mm wide cover narrow boards to large panels — the widest PCB you run decides the chassis.

Are the racks certified?

The SMT ESD Magazine Rack series (models G6801–G6805) carries a CE Certificate of Conformity and RoHS compliance per the IEC 62321 test series. Copies are attached to any inquiry reply on request.

Email info@smthelp.com or message us on WhatsApp with three things: PCB width, number of racks needed, and where they will run (bench, loader line, oven side). Quotes go out within 24 hours on working days.
