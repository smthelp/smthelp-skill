The Print-Shop Reality

Every un-stored stencil costs you twice

Once in damaged foils and bent frames — and again in the five minutes an operator spends hunting for the right stencil at every changeover.

Stencils leaning against walls

Frame edges get knocked, foils crease, and paste-side damage shows up as print defects two weeks later on the SPI.

Changeover hunts

The stencil for the next job is "somewhere in the rack" — or in the cleaning room, or behind the printer. Line waits while someone searches.

Floor space bleeding out

Flat storage tables and scattered frames eat square meters you could use for a second line or a kitting bench.

Audit findings

5S and ISO auditors photograph cluttered stencil corners first. Upright slotted racks make stencil control visually obvious.

Wrong-stencil risk

Without individual slots and spacing, look-alike frames get mixed — and the wrong foil reaches the printer.

Cleaning-room ping-pong

No dedicated route from printer to cleaner to storage means stencils travel on carts, trolleys and arms all day.

Store · Move · Retrieve

One trolley, four steps, zero stencil handling

The rack is not furniture — it is part of the stencil workflow between printer, cleaning and storage.

STEP 1 · SLOT Stencils stand upright in individual wire slots — no stacking, no frame-to-frame contact
STEP 2 · SPACE 25 / 35 / 40 mm slot pitch keeps foils separated so paste residue never transfers between frames
STEP 3 · ROLL Swivel casters move the whole rack to the printer or cleaning room — stencils never leave the rack
STEP 4 · RE-CONFIGURE Clip-fixed shelves reposition in minutes when stencil formats or volumes change

FAQ

Before you order

Which rack suits framed mesh stencils vs laser foils?

Framed mesh stencils are thicker and heavier — Type B (40 mm pitch, 150 kg shelves) or Type C add-on bays fit them best. Fine laser foils store efficiently at 25 mm pitch (Type A or B). Send your stencil thickness and we confirm the pitch before quoting.

Will these racks hold my stencil size?

Chassis depths run 500–750 mm and heights 1200–2200 mm, covering common stencil frames. The largest frame you store decides the chassis — that is why our quote starts from your stencil dimensions, not a catalog page.

How mobile are the racks — can one operator move a full rack?

All four types mount swivel casters; a loaded 4-tier rack rolls comfortably on flat EPA flooring. For the heaviest framed-stencil loads, Type B offers steel-bearing 3-inch casters.

What does assembly involve?

Type A/B/D use clip-fixed shelves — posts, collars and wire shelves lock by hand. Type C bays ship knock-down with a wire-lock frame and assemble with basic hand tools. An illustrated assembly sheet ships with every order.

How do the racks ship overseas?

Flat-pack by default — far more racks per container than assembled shipping, and lower freight damage risk. Export cartons with strapping; assembly on site takes one technician under an hour per rack.

How do I get a quote?

Email info@smthelp.com or message us on WhatsApp with three things: stencil frame size, number of stencils to store, and your target layout (beside printer vs central storage wall). Quotes go out within 24 hours on working days.
