# Content rules

## Evidence (from learn-SMT-in-3-hours + product AGENTS.md)
- Source priority: Southern Machinery manual/spec/brochure > Excel/quotation structure (never prices) > photos/video (appearance only) > old HTML/third-party (background only).
- Every spec row carries an evidence label: `v` Verified source (current SM document), `c` To be confirmed (conflict, single old source, or customer-specific), `t` Concept view (diagrams).
- Conflicts: one Open-points row per parameter with value A, value B, what it means for the buyer, status To be confirmed.
- Never publish: price, discount, lead time, stock, warranty, payment terms, customer names/orders, contacts from old quotes, bank details, CE/ISO claims without certificate, throw-rate/yield/ROI percentages, OEM affiliation. Third-party brands identify compatible equipment only; add a non-affiliation line in FAQ and footer.
- Compatibility: only what the manual states is "fit"; everything else is "ask first" with the facts needed (machine model, feeder/part series, widths/quantity, destination voltage, photo/video).
- Learning section: 30-minute study route (5 blocks, each with a completion check), fault-to-check sequence starting with safe state and ending with escalation, maintenance card quoting the manual. Not a substitute for the manual.

## Dan Koe six-step blog method (titles never name the steps)
Each article = 6 paragraphs in this order:
1. Attention - a concrete scene from the shop floor in the first sentence.
2. The real pain - why it costs money/time and why the usual suspect is wrong.
3. Reusable method - a rule or routine the reader can apply without buying anything.
4. The product - how this product carries the method; only verified functions.
5. Return habit - a recurring check/record/review that brings the reader back.
6. Next step - exactly what to send (model, series, quantity, photo) and where (info@smthelp.com / WhatsApp / form).
Eight angles that work: misattributed fault, incoming/used inspection, after-repair gate, shared reference ends arguments, wear visible early, scaling a maintenance room/station, calendar vs crisis, what to send before buying.

## Tone
Engineering, concise, no hype adjectives, numbers only from sources. English source; the EN page is the canonical SEO version.

## SEO / GEO
- File name and title: product + model + main search phrase; title <= ~70 chars, description 150-170 chars with key specs.
- `ans_p` one-paragraph answer and Quick facts list are written to be quoted by AI answer engines: who makes it, what it is, what it does, how, who uses it.
- JSON-LD graph: Organization, WebPage (+speakable), BreadcrumbList, Product (+additionalProperty, disambiguatingDescription listing open points), HowTo (study steps), FAQPage, VideoObject list, Blog with 8 BlogPosting. hreflang for 7 languages + x-default.
