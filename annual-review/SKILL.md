# Annual Review

## Purpose

Prepare a professional annual review that strengthens VIP customer relationships and identifies next-year opportunities.

## Workflow

1. Gather customer profile, purchased equipment, installed lines, service records, support cases, training, spare parts, production impact, and pending needs.
2. Summarize cooperation highlights and measurable value; separate verified data from assumptions.
3. Identify next-year opportunities such as additional insertion machines, line expansion, board handling, AOI, wave soldering, spare parts, or service upgrades.
4. Keep the tone appreciative, strategic, and partnership-oriented.
5. End with a meeting CTA to review the next automation plan.

## Southern Machinery Rules

- Keep customer information confidential.
- Do not invent order values, service records, or production results.
- Default to English unless the user requests another language.
- Include assumptions and items requiring human confirmation.
