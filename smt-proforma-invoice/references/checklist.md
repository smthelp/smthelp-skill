# PI release checklist (after qa_pi.py shows 0 ERROR)
1. PI No. follows the rule and is not used before (check pis/ and sent mail).
2. PO No. and buyer legal name exactly as on the PO; ship-to address complete.
3. Each line: model, description, qty and unit match the PO; price matches the quotation or Jason's decision.
4. Charges, discount and total match what Jason approved; amount in words matches.
5. Incoterm + place, insurance and duties wording agree (EXW/FCA/FOB: buyer insures; CIF/CIP: we insure; D-terms: our risk; DDP: duties ours).
6. Payment adds to 100 % and has a trigger; delivery time has a start point.
7. Bank block = JPMorgan HK details, beneficiary = company name; CNY PI shows WeChat/Alipay line.
8. No supplier names or supplier model codes, no TO_CONFIRM, no draft banner, PDF pages look right.
